# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a place where anyone can ask a beginner question,
propose an idea, or disagree about a design decision without being made to feel
unwelcome.

Everyone taking part — maintainers, contributors and people opening issues —
agrees to make participation a harassment-free experience for all, regardless of
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Critique the code, not the person.
- Accept that maintainers may say no, and explain why when you do.
- Remember that "obvious" is relative — this project is explicitly beginner friendly.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind
- Sexualised language or imagery
- Publishing other people's private information without permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit messages — and whenever someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour by opening a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/Msheez/ai-prompt-toolkit/security/advisories/new)
(it is the quietest private channel this repository has) or by contacting the
maintainer directly on GitHub: [@Msheez](https://github.com/Msheez).

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers
will respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

Maintainers may warn, temporarily block, or permanently ban anyone whose
behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful, and
may remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that do not align with
this Code of Conduct.
